# Scan to File

A fun "scan to file" animation that runs in your browser. Pick a photo and
watch it get scanned, digitized into 1s and 0s, folded into a file card,
and fed to the file monster.

▶️ **Try it:** https://shaff3874.github.io/scan-to-file/

## How to try it

1. Open the link above in **Chrome or Safari**. Opening it inside WhatsApp,
   Telegram or a file viewer app may stop the photo picker from working.
2. Tap **Choose image** and pick a photo.
3. Tap **▶ Scan & upload** to play the animation, or **Batch mode (fast)** for
   a quick version. Turn the sound on for the effects.

**Nothing is uploaded.** Everything runs on your own phone or computer.
The "UPLOADING" bar is part of the animation.

## Make your own copy

**Option A: your own live page (GitHub)**
1. Tap **Fork** at the top of this repository to copy it to your account.
2. In your copy, go to **Settings → Pages**.
3. Set Source to **Deploy from a branch**, choose **main** and **/ (root)**,
   then tap **Save**.
4. After 1 to 2 minutes your page is live at
   `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/scan-to-file/`.

**Option B: just the file**
Tap **Code → Download ZIP**, unzip it, and open `index.html` in a browser.

## Customize it

Everything is in one file, `index.html`. Search the file for the text you
want to change, for example `SCAN TO FILE`, `DIGITIZING YOUR MASTERPIECE`
or `FEEDING THE FILE MONSTER`.

## Want a real assignment-submission app?

This page is an animation only and does not store files. To collect real
submissions, connect it to something that stores files, such as a Google
Form with a file-upload question, and show this animation as a fun
